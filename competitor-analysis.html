How competitors build and use their websites — and what that means for Shopify + Astro

I checked what each competitor actually runs: platform, domain layout, checkout and apps, B2B pages, trust proof, consultation funnels, reviews, languages and AI-crawler policy. Everything here was read from their live sites, headers, robots.txt and sitemaps. Nothing is from memory or third-party "tech lookup" tools.

Summary

The market answers your platform question clearly. Shopify is the default. The brands that left it are venture-funded D2C companies with their own engineering teams and their own back ends. The brands most like SGVP (doctor-led, content-heavy, institutional) split a content site from a shop. That split is the Shopify + Astro plan.

Adding an Astro content front end copies what Jiva, Planet Ayurveda and Arya Vaidya Sala do, which are the doctor-led and institutional brands, and improves on it (see the domain patterns below). Who went headless, and how your plan differs Kapiva, Nat Habit and Man Matters built custom Next.js storefronts on their own back ends. All three are venture-funded D2C brands with in-house engineering teams, mobile apps and subscription systems, and they run their own cart and checkout. The more relevant case is Arya Vaidya Sala: a trust-owned Ayurvedic institution like SGVP, with a WordPress content site and a headless Next.js shop, apparently on a Magento back end. It shows an institution can run a split, headless setup. Your plan does the same with less to maintain, because Shopify keeps the checkout, payments and orders, which cost the most to build and run yourself.

Domain patterns — four ways competitors lay it out

Each pattern, who uses it, and how it compares with the plan for SGVP.

AOne domain, one Shopify store
Who: Charak, Zandu, Dr Vaidya's, Kerala Ayurveda, Vedix, Sri Sri Tattva, Organic India, Anveshan, Two Brothers, SGVP today
  • Products, blog and pages all on brand.com, served by the Shopify theme
  • Simplest to run; all SEO authority in one place
  • Limited by what the theme allows: schema, speed, language routes
BContent site + shop subdomain
Who: Jiva (store.jiva.com), Planet Ayurveda (store.), Arya Vaidya Sala (shop.)
  • Doctor-led and institutional brands: content, clinics and consultation on the root; products on a subdomain
  • Content ranks strongly (Planet's 100+ disease library; Jiva's disease pages)
  • Weakness: product pages sit on the subdomain, so they get only part of the root's authority
CShop on root, content on subdomains
Who: Himalaya
  • Shopify store on himalayawellness.in
  • Separate subdomains: herbfinder., healthhelp., researchpapers., storelocator., careers.
  • Works for a national brand with huge authority. For a smaller brand it spreads content authority thin.
DFully custom headless
Who: Kapiva, Nat Habit, Man Matters
  • Everything on one domain, rendered by Next.js
  • Best speed and full control
  • They build and run checkout, payments and order systems themselves. Needs an engineering team.
Where the SGVP plan sits: pattern B, with its weakness fixed The plan is Astro on sgvpaarogyam.com for everything Google sees, including product pages, and Shopify on shop.sgvpaarogyam.com for checkout only. That gives you the content strength of Jiva and Planet Ayurveda, while product pages stay on the root domain and keep its full authority. It also avoids the engineering load of Kapiva's pattern, because Shopify still runs the cart, payments and orders.

What to take from this

  • GA4 + GTM is universal. All 15 stores in this table except SGVP have it.
  • One-click checkout is now standard, including among Ayurveda pharma brands. GoKwik is on Charak, Dr Vaidya's, Kerala Ayurveda, Zandu, Two Brothers, Sri Sri Tattva and Vedix; Fastrr on Anveshan, Charak and Sri Sri Tattva. It pre-fills the address, scores COD risk and cuts RTO. For a COD-heavy medicine store, test it before any redesign.
  • WhatsApp marketing tools (Wati, LimeChat, MoEngage) power the refill and abandoned-cart flows in the digital plan.
  • Microsoft Clarity (free session recordings) is on four sites. Add it; it shows exactly where visitors get stuck.

Check before building Astro

  • Theme apps such as Judge.me widgets, one-click checkout buttons and WhatsApp bubbles don't appear on a headless front end on their own. Each has to be added in code.
  • Ask GoKwik, Fastrr, Judge.me and your WhatsApp tool whether they support headless Shopify before committing. Most offer APIs or scripts for it, but confirm for each one.
  • If a must-have app doesn't support headless, that is the one real reason to stay on the theme longer.

Method and limits

How the data was collected

  • Homepages, response headers and sitemaps fetched directly for 12 sites; platform identified from markup, headers and generator tags
  • Nine sites (Himalaya, Charak, Dr Vaidya's, Jiva, Man Matters, Kerala Ayurveda, Zandu, Arya Vaidya Sala, Planet Ayurveda) were first read through a web-fetch tool while the office filter blocked them, then re-checked directly once the network allowed, including their shop subdomains. Platforms, apps and schema in this report come from that direct check.
  • Page sitemaps searched for B2B, gifting, quality and certification URLs

What this does not cover

  • Competitor page speed is deliberately left out. The speed work focuses on SGVP's own pages.
  • Traffic, rankings and revenue. These need a paid SEO tool (Ahrefs or Similarweb), which isn't connected in this session.
  • Homepage-level app detection only. Apps loaded only on product or cart pages may be missed.
  • Dabur's store platform was not identified; only its corporate site was checked.
  • Everything reflects 30 Sep 2026. Sites change often.
