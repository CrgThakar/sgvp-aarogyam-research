Section 1 · Business model

Own brand, manufactured by SGVP Aarogyam

Third-party (contract) manufacturing: SGVP makes the product, your brand name goes on the pack. This is how most new Ayurvedic brands in India start.

Third-party manufacturing (recommended start)Loan licence
Who holds the licenceSGVP Aarogyam (existing Gujarat FDCA / AYUSH licence)You — own licence issued on SGVP's premises
Label"Manufactured by: SGVP Aarogyam… Marketed by: [your company]""Manufactured by: [your company] at SGVP Aarogyam premises"
SetupLow; no licence needed to startApply to Gujarat FDCA (AYUSH) with SGVP consent letter; 2–4 months
ControlSGVP decides formulation & QC; you approve artworkYou own formulation and registration; easier to switch factory
Best forRelabelling SGVP's existing formulasProprietary formulas at scale

Steps

  1. Company + trademark

    Pvt Ltd / LLP / proprietorship, GST. Trademark Class 5 (Ayurvedic medicines), Class 29 (ghee/dairy), Class 3 (cosmetics). ~₹4,500 per class, 6–12 months; ™ usable from filing.

  2. Product list

    (a) SGVP's existing formulations relabelled — 4–6 weeks; or (b) your own formulation — SGVP must get each proprietary medicine approved by the state licensing authority (Rule 158-B), adds 2–4 months.

  3. Quotation

    MOQ typically 500–2,000 units per SKU. Ask for raw material, conversion, packaging and testing costs separately. Ex-factory ≈ 25–40% of MRP for Ayurvedic tablets.

  4. Manufacturing agreement

    Brand exclusivity, formula confidentiality, quality specs + batch COA, artwork/brand ownership (you), product-complaint liability, MOQ, payment terms, exit clause.

  5. Packaging & label (Rule 161)

    Name, ingredients with quantities and reference text, "Ayurvedic Proprietary Medicine", net content, batch, mfg/exp, licence no., manufacturer + marketer name and address, MRP, AYUSH warnings. No "cure / permanent / control" wording (Drugs & Magic Remedies Act).

  6. First batch

    30–45 days from artwork approval; released with a Certificate of Analysis.

  7. Sell

    As marketer of ASU medicines you need no drug licence to sell online or to distributors. Food-positioned products (ghee, supplements) need an FSSAI licence in the brand owner's name.

Ask SGVP Aarogyam in the first meeting

  • Licences held — GMP under Schedule T? Copies needed for Amazon/1mg and distributor trust
  • Do they already do third-party manufacturing for other brands?
  • MOQ per SKU; price at 1k / 5k / 10k units; is packaging included?
  • Loan licence later — will they give the premises consent letter?
  • Who handles stability data and shelf-life declaration?
  • Co-branding: fee for "Manufactured by SGVP Aarogyam" on the pack? (It is legally required and an asset in Gujarat)

Starting budget (10 SKUs × 1,000 units)

  • Product cost: ₹8–15 L
  • Packaging design + printing: ₹1–2 L
  • Trademark, company, GST, CA: ₹30–50 K
  • Lab testing / COAs: usually included; ₹50 K if external
  • Total ≈ ₹10–18 L before marketing

What to take care of

  1. Answer Amul A2 on the pack

    Gaushala, batch report, Bilona ratio — on the front label, not a website page. Without it ₹1,800 vs ₹1,400 loses in Gujarat.

  2. Supply before sales

    70 people can sell far more than the gaushala can make. Confirm daily litres, then release territories one at a time.

  3. One price list everywhere

    Same MRP online and offline; same trade margin for distributors and marketplaces. If retailers see Blinkit cheaper, they stop stocking you.

  4. Claims discipline

    No "cures", "good for heart / diabetes". FSSAI and the Magic Remedies Act both apply, and the Ayurvedic house makes you more visible to regulators, not less.

  5. Name and trademark first

    Class 29 filed before the first pack is printed. Decide whether SGVP's name goes on the front (asset in Gujarat) or only as manufacturer on the back (better for Mumbai and export).

  6. Quality consistency

    A gaushala brand dies on one bad batch going viral. Batch COA, retained samples, 12-month stability study.

  7. Working capital

    Distributors take 21–30 days credit; ghee ties up raw material for 45 days. Fund ~90 days of stock.

  8. Online and field must not fight

    Attribute every online order to a field territory (below) so the team earns from online growth instead of resisting it.
